Privacy Policy — Voice Translator: Speak & Text

Package app.voicetranslator.translate · Publisher: Zoopet · Last updated 13 October 2026

This policy explains what the Voice Translator: Speak & Text app ("the app") does with information. The app has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own.

1. Text you translate

  • On-device translation. When the languages you use are downloaded, translation runs on your phone with Google ML Kit. The text is not sent to us or to Google for translation.
  • Online translation (optional). If the setting "Use online translation when needed" is on (you choose this during setup and can change it in Settings) and a language is not downloaded, the text you translate is sent over an encrypted connection to the MyMemory translation service operated by Translated S.r.l. (Italy). MyMemory states that it stores submitted text and may share it with its machine-translation partners; see MyMemory terms. We send no name, e-mail, account or device identifier with the text; MyMemory receives your IP address as part of the connection. Each result in the app is labelled "On-device" or "Online" so you know which was used. Choose "Offline only" to make sure text never leaves your phone.

2. Voice

When you tap a microphone, audio is processed by your phone's speech-recognition service (on Android this is usually provided by Google). Where your phone supports on-device recognition for the language, the app asks for it first. The app does not record, store or upload audio itself. Spoken output uses your phone's text-to-speech voices on the device. The microphone permission is requested only when you first tap a microphone, and you can revoke it at any time in Android settings.

3. Photos

Photos you take with the system camera or choose with the system photo picker are read on your phone with Google ML Kit text recognition. Photos are not uploaded. A photo taken in the app is kept only in the app's temporary cache and overwritten by the next one. Recognised text you choose to translate follows section 1.

4. History, favourites and settings

Translations you make (if "Save history" is on), favourites, phrasebook translations and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. You can delete single entries, clear history, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

5. Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use Unity Ads through AdMob mediation. In regions where it is required (for example the EEA, UK and some US states), a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options. Ad providers may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, and app interaction and diagnostics data, as described in Google's partner policy and Unity's privacy policy. No ads are shown after you buy "Remove ads".

6. Purchases

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play or Samsung Galaxy Store. The store processes payment; the app only receives a confirmation of ownership, which it checks on your phone. We do not receive your payment details.

7. Google ML Kit

ML Kit may send Google diagnostic and usage information (for example device and app information, performance metrics and a per-installation identifier) to maintain and improve its features, as described in ML Kit's data disclosure. It does not send the text or images you translate. Translation models are downloaded from Google when you choose to download a language.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Your choices and rights

You can switch to Offline only, turn off history, revoke the microphone permission, change ad consent in Privacy options, reset your advertising ID in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access or delete data held by third parties named above; contact them directly or write to us and we will help.
